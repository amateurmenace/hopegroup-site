Graduate medical education runs on documentation: accreditation requirements, policies, handbooks, evaluations, and recruitment. AI can take real weight off the people who manage it, but only inside the rules that make medical education trustworthy.

This six-week course, running January 14 through February 18, 2027, walks GME program administrators, coordinators, and leaders through identifying the right tools, automating routine tasks, streamlining policy and data work, using AI in recruitment and branding, and building ethical, compliant workflows that align with institutional policy.
