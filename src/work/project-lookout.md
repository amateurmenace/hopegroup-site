Large organizations have business development teams watching procurement portals and grant databases every day. Most small businesses and nonprofits do not, and the gap shows up as money that goes to whoever could afford to look.

Project Lookout closes that gap. It runs many small AI agents in parallel across more than a hundred public portals and sources, extracts each opportunity into structured data with a confidence score, verifies that it is real and still open, and ranks it against your organization's actual profile. Then it hands the list to a person, with a draft ready if you want one.

It was designed and built with the Black Economic Council of Massachusetts and is deployed with institutional partners across the Commonwealth, including the City of Boston. It exists because the organizations that have always deserved this kind of infrastructure never had access to it.
