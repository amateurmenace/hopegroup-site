Most customer relationship tools were built for sales teams with quotas. The organizations we work with keep relationships for other reasons: a client who needs a follow-up, a funder who asked a question, a neighbor who came to a workshop and wants the next one.

Driftwood is a CRM for that kind of work. It keeps the people, the conversations, and the next steps in one place, adds AI assistance where it saves real time, and makes a simple promise about your data: you own what you put in, we do not train on it, and you can take it with you.

Driftwood is in the lab now with early partners. If your organization wants to help shape it, we would like to hear from you.
