In May 2026 Rev. Hope joined a United Nations panel on strategizing AI in education, bringing the lab's on-the-ground experience with Boston small businesses, nonprofits, and classrooms to a room of national policymakers. The message was the same one on this website: the difference is not the technology. It is who builds it, who it is built for, and what values guide it.
