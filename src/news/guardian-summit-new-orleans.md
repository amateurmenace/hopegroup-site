Rev. Christopher Hope joined the Guardian Summit in New Orleans, a convening of faith leaders, technologists, and justice advocates working at the intersection of AI, ethics, and community. His remarks drew on the lab's work with Boston small businesses, nonprofits, and public agencies, and on the idea at the center of everything we build: a Digital Beloved Community.
