Registration is open for *Integrating AI in Medical Education Administration*, a six-week live virtual course led by the lab's Compliance + Leadership Lead Trainer, Kaytlyn Hope, with guest trainers.

The course runs Thursdays from 3:30 to 5:00 PM Eastern, January 14 through February 18, 2027, and is built for GME program administrators and coordinators, GME leaders, medical education managers and support staff, and anyone interested in ethical AI adoption in medical education administration. Participants leave able to identify the right AI tools for GME work, automate routine tasks, streamline policy and documentation, use AI responsibly in recruitment and branding, and build ethical, compliant workflows.

The cost is $149 per participant.
