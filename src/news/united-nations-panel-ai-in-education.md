Rev. Christopher Hope co-hosted and presented at a United Nations Science, Technology and Innovation Forum side event, joining a panel on strategizing AI in education. He brought the lab's on-the-ground experience with Boston classrooms, small businesses, and nonprofits to a room of policymakers from member nations, and made the case that equitable technology in education is decided by who builds the tools and who they are built for.
